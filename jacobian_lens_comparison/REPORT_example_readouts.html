Jacobian lens vs. logit lens, on the organism

How the two readout methods behave on the same secret-gender organisms, focused entirely on the organism model itself (no base-model control here). Section 1 summarizes the aggregate auditor-accuracy numbers across all three sources that now exist for this comparison. Section 2 drills into four hand-picked prompts, comparing the two methods' token-level readouts side by side.
